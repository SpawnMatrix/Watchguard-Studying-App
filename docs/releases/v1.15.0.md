# Version 1.15.0 — Exam readiness, a lab dashboard and plainer screens

The Progress page now answers the question learners bring to it: *am I ready for the exam, and what should I study next?* Lab Exercises opens on a dashboard instead of an empty panel. Several screens lose debug-style wording and wrapping at common laptop widths.

## Exam readiness on Progress & Admin

A new **Exam readiness** panel scores the exam for the current learning track:

- **Categories:** it shows the six NSE categories on the Local Firebox track, or the five N10-009 domains on the Network+ track.
- **Scores:** each category shows its exam weight, how many of its questions you have answered, and your score.
- **Latest answer:** each question counts once, by your most recent answer. A question you missed early and have since answered correctly counts as known, and repeating an easy question doesn't inflate the score.
- **Minimum answers:** a category shows no score until it has five answered questions. The overall estimate waits until every category has five, because a figure built from a couple of lucky answers would mislead.
- **Weighted estimate:** the estimate is weighted by the published outline. For NSE, it's compared with WatchGuard's 75% pass mark: "65% weighted by the exam outline. The exam needs 75%, so you are 10 points short."
  - Each score bar marks the 75% line.
  - CompTIA reports a scaled score rather than a percentage, so no pass claim is made for Network+.
- **Study next:** the panel names the category where the most exam weight is still being missed. For example, Networking and NAT at 57% outranks Authentication and VPNs at 48%, because it is worth 25% of the exam rather than 15%.

WatchGuard Cloud has no published outline, so the panel says so and points to the other tracks. Study Home's topic card links to the panel.

## Topics to review, in plain language

The old **Critical Weakness Tracker** listed every topic with even one wrong answer as a "Vulnerability" with a "Remediation Mandatory" badge. After a few dozen answers, that was nearly every topic.

It is now **Topics to review**. It lists only topics below 75% on your latest answers with at least three answered, weakest first, as "3 of 9 right · 33%". It follows the current track.

## Other Progress changes

- **Stat cards:** "Quiz attempts: 135 / 210 Correct" wrapped onto several lines. The card now reads "Questions answered: 210", with "135 answered correctly" underneath. The same cards on Study Home get the fix.
- **Study report:** the card and report dropped "Executive Performance Audit", "Compile & Run Audit Analysis", "Recommended Study Remediation Labs" and a pinging dot. They now read **Study report**, **Build my study report**, **Recommended labs** and **Summary**.
- **Lab recommendations:** the study report listed labs in catalog order for any topic with a single miss. Study Home shows the first two, so it nearly always suggested Labs 1 and 3. Topics are now ordered by accuracy and the labs follow them, so a learner weakest in BOVPN and Mobile VPN is pointed to Labs 16 and 15.

## Lab Exercises opens on a lab dashboard

Before a lab is opened, the stage used to say only "Pick a lab to start". It now shows:

- labs complete and in progress, with a progress bar
- a **Resume** button for the lab in progress, with its steps done
- the next unstarted lab
- every hands-on lab with its status

At 1024px wide, the catalog's sort and category menus stack instead of truncating to "By numb" and "All categ".

## Practice Quiz

The strip above the quiz read "Spaced repetition is prioritising NAT (40%), BOVPN (38%) · 7 concepts retained at box 5", in monospace. It also listed topics from other tracks and topics that practice wasn't actually favouring.

It now shows up to three topics from the current track that practice is favouring, as chips ordered weakest first. It also shows how many questions are learned, meaning answered correctly four times in a row.

## Flashcards, Q&A Desk and layout

- **Flashcards:**
  - "Reset All Progress" sat under a card about study guide links. It is now a **Clear mastered** button beside the mastered count, and its confirmation says it clears all three tracks.
  - The stat cards now describe the deck and how to study a card.
  - "All Syllabuses", "Setup Domain" and "Core Exam Syllabus" are now plain topic names.
- **Q&A Desk:** "Syllabus Item #3", "Syllabus Reference mode" and "AI Tutor Mode Authorized" are now "Note 3", "Study notes" and "AI tutor available". The AI-off message says where to add your own key.
- **Sidebar:** labels such as "Progress & Admin" no longer wrap at 1024px.
- **Stat cards:** the decorative icons are hidden below 1280px, so labels have room.

## Lab simulator

The simulated Firebox's default policies now include **WatchGuard Certificate Portal** (TCP 4126), which the Study Guide lists among the policies the setup wizard creates (pp. 14 and 217).

## Verification

- **New tests:**
  - **Exam readiness:** no scores before five answers; every Local Firebox question counts towards one category; the latest answer counts once; the weighted estimate and focus category are correct; Network+ stays separate with no pass mark; the summary, focus and shortfall text render.
  - **Topics to review:** latest answer per question on the current track; only topics with enough answers under 75%; no "remediation" or "vulnerability" wording.
  - **Stat cards:** the questions-answered card no longer renders "x / y Correct".
  - **Lab dashboard:** a new learner is offered Lab 1; a lab in progress is offered before the next unstarted lab; hands-on lab names aren't repeated; every-lab-complete state.
  - **Study report:** labs for the weakest topic come first.
- `npm run lint` is clean, and 331 tests pass across 38 files. `engine.test.ts` and `phase2.test.ts` are unchanged.
- **Browser:** checked in the dev preview with synthetic practice history in local storage.
  - Progress at 1440px and 375px: the readiness table fits without horizontal scrolling on a phone.
  - Lab Exercises at 1024px, with a lab in progress.
  - The practice strip and Study Home's lab suggestions.

## Compatibility

No stored data changes shape. Readiness is calculated from the practice history the app already keeps, and the lab dashboard reads the existing lab progress. Nothing is migrated, and existing progress shows up immediately.
