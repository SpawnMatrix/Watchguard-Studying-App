# Version 1.7.0 — Labs you can actually work through

The Labs tab listed all 20 Lab Book exercises, but a lab was a page of instructions and a "Proceed" button. It could be clicked through in a minute without learning anything, most learners have no Firebox to follow along on, and several problems made it feel broken:

- **The step button could be out of sight.** It sat at the far right of the footer, so a narrow window, or another window overlapping the browser, hid it.
- **Finishing a lab sent you to the Admin tab,** which a learner without the admin role cannot use.
- **Progress was never saved.** Leaving the tab or reloading restarted the lab from step 1.

## What changed

- **A checkpoint question on every step (81 in total).** Each one asks about the step you just read: the menu path, the value to enter, or what you should see when it worked. You must answer correctly to complete the step. A wrong answer explains why and lets you try again. Every answer comes from that step's own instructions, so the checkpoints drill exactly what the Lab Book covers, and they are useful with or without hardware.
- **A step tracker.** It shows which steps are done and lets you revisit any of them. Later steps unlock in order.
- **Progress saves per step and syncs with your account** (the new `watchguard-lab-progress-v1` study key, validated on the server like the rest). The catalogue shows each lab as "2/5 steps" or "Completed".
- **A completion screen.** It shows how many checkpoints you got right first time, names the steps worth revisiting, and offers the next lab, a review, or a restart from scratch. You stay on the lab instead of being moved to Admin.
- **Controls you can always see.** The completion button comes first in the footer, a Previous step button sits beside it, and the footer wraps on narrow screens. The lab header now shows what kit the lab needs.
- **Wider catalogue filters.** The sort and category dropdowns were squeezed to about 70px, which cut their labels off ("By num"); they now fill the column.
- **Clearer troubleshooting help.** "Stuck on this step on a real Firebox?" now says when AI is turned off and its answer is general guidance, and marks its log lines as illustrative.

The checkpoints were checked for the answer-length tell: the correct option is the longest in 25% of questions where length is noticeable, and a test holds that between 15% and 35%.

No database migration or deployment configuration change is required. Labs already recorded as complete still show as completed.
