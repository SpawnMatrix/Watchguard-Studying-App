# Version 1.15.4 — Show the diagram size toggle only when it does something

The **Fit diagram / Readable size** button on topology diagrams switches between shrinking the diagram to its panel and drawing it at native width with sideways scrolling. When the panel is already wider than the diagram, both modes draw the same picture, so on a wide desktop the button changed its label and nothing else and looked broken. On a 1920px screen the Topology Lab panel is 1127px and every current scene is 850–1070px wide, so that was every diagram.

- The diagram now measures the width it is drawn in and offers the toggle only when the diagram is wider than that. Laptops and phones, where the choice matters, keep it; at 1366px the panel is 803px and at phone width 341px, and both still show a working toggle.
- The measurement follows the panel as the window resizes, and happens before paint, so a wide screen never flashes the button.
- The "Scroll to explore wider diagrams" hint goes with the toggle. A diagram with no packet animation and nothing to resize leaves the toolbar out entirely rather than showing an empty strip.
- Applies everywhere the diagram appears: Topology Lab previews, topology practice questions and the broken-network lab.

No migration, no data-contract or deploy-script change. The branch is delivered by pull request and does not deploy itself.
