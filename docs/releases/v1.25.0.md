# Version 1.25.0 — A daily flashcard deck that knows when to bring cards back

Practice questions have used Leitner spaced repetition since 1.2.0: five
boxes, intervals of 0, 1, 3, 7 and 21 days, and a miss drops straight to
box 1. Flashcards never joined in. A card was either marked mastered by hand
or not, and the studio was a 315-card list to page through. Nothing told a
learner which cards to look at today, and a card answered correctly last week
was treated the same as one never seen.

## Due today

The Flashcards section now opens on **Due today**, next to the existing
**Browse all**.

- The deck holds the cards whose interval has elapsed, oldest first, then up
  to **10 new cards a day**, so a first session is ten cards rather than the
  whole track. The allowance is counted per local day across all categories,
  so switching category cannot reset it.
- Reveal a card, then grade it **Again** or **Knew it**, or press `1` / `2`.
  "Knew it" moves the card up one box. "Again" returns it to box 1.
- **A missed card goes to the end of today's deck, not straight back.** The
  first build re-showed it immediately, because due reviews sorted ahead of new
  cards, and that tests whether you remember the answer you read three seconds
  ago. Box 1 cards now come last. If one is the only card left, it is still
  shown.
- Box 5 is retained and leaves the schedule, as it does for questions.
- **Cards marked mastered by hand are left out.** That choice was the
  learner's, and the schedule does not argue with it. Browse all, Mark as
  Mastered, category filters and search work as before.
- When nothing is due, the studio says so and when the next card comes back,
  with a way into Browse all.

The intervals and box rule come from `src/engine/srs.ts`, so a change there
moves both schedules together.

## Storage

A new study key, **`watchguard-flashcard-srs-v1`**, holds each reviewed card's
box, due time and first-review time. It syncs with the account. The server
validates it in the shared schema: numeric ids, boxes 1–5, finite times, at
most 2,000 entries. It is listed in `docs/privacy.md`, and the inventory test
added in 1.24.0 failed until it was.

Unlike the sandbox key, this one records **when** someone studied: the first
review time is what spends a day's new-card allowance, and the due time
encodes the last review. The privacy entry says so. It reveals no more than the
answer history in `watchguard-study-progress-v1` already does.

## Not done here

The Study Home page could show "N cards due". It would mean the home screen
importing the flashcard data, about 100 kB, into the bundle every visitor loads
first, which works against the bundle-size work coming next. Left for after
that, when the count can come from a lazily-loaded module.

## Verification

- `npm run lint` clean. `npx vitest run`: 52 files, **448 tests**, green.
  - Engine: box progression, box-5 retirement, ordering, the per-day
    allowance across categories and its reset at midnight, missed cards going
    last, mastered cards excluded, validation, and room for every flashcard.
  - Render: a new learner opens on "Due today (10)" with "10 left today: 0 to
    review, 10 new.", and a fully scheduled deck says the day is done and when
    the next card returns.
- Driven in a production build: graded card 1 "Knew it", then card 2 "Again"
  with the `1` key. Card 1 went to box 2 due tomorrow and card 2 to box 1,
  both persisted. After the ordering fix, missing card 1 showed card 2 next,
  with the count at "10 left today: 1 to review, 9 new". A full reload kept the
  deck at 9 left, on the same card.

Delivered by pull request, stacked on 1.24.0. No migration: a snapshot without
the key just means no flashcard has been reviewed yet.
